# Commercial Gap Analysis — Zethic

Date: 2026-09-20  
Status: proposal only; no production-site change or deploy performed.

## Current public proposition

The current homepage positions Zethic as an evidence-based operations governance consultancy. It explains a cycle of diagnosis, structuring, supervised operation and evidence-based closing, but it does not expose the catalogued audiovisual and digital-organization offers.

## Main gaps

1. No explicit path for the three commercial fronts under evaluation: content production, digital organization, and Cyber/GRC diagnosis.
2. No problem-specific CTA.
3. The contact phone must not be the only conversion route until end-to-end service is validated.
4. No authorized portfolio sample by offer.
5. The wide internal service map is not suitable for a single public homepage.
6. Prices must not be published before cost, capacity and margin approval.

## Proposed information architecture — pending human approval

- Home: evidence proposition and three pathways.
- Content: editing, authority portraits and content-day formats.
- Operations: digital organization and channel governance.
- Cyber/GRC: diagnosis, risk matrix, responsibilities and prioritized plan.
- Contact: tested form/e-mail, expected response time and privacy notice.
